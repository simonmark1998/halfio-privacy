Half.io Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Half.io handles information when you use the Half.io game.

Information we collect

Half.io does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information. Half.io does not use advertising, analytics, or social-media tracking services.

Local game progress

Your level progress, scores, and stars are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. This information is not transmitted to us and is not synchronized between devices. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress.

Third-party services

Half.io is distributed through app stores. Google Play and other platform providers may process information according to their own privacy policies when you download or use their services.

Children's privacy

Half.io does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this URL, with its effective date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support email shown on the Half.io Google Play listing.
